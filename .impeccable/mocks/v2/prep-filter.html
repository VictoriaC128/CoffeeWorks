What each one does to the cup
Water temp
Hotter: fuller, sweeter. Cooler: brighter, lighter. Shaded band: light washed 92–96 °C.
Hardness
Soft: sparkling acid, light body. Hard or KH over ~50: heavier, duller fruit.
Grind
Finer: slower drawdown, more body. Too fine: bitter, stalls.
Dose · water
More coffee or less water: stronger cup. Ratio updates as you change either.
